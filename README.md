# 牛马检测器｜Niuma Detector

**检测班味，拯救人味。**

Niuma Detector is a playful AI camera web app that detects office fatigue, assesses workspace wellness, and generates humorous health, diet, movement, music, and recovery suggestions.

It turns your workspace into a retro game-style diagnostic interface: scan the room, calculate your 牛马指数, check your 人味余额, and generate a shareable social card.

## What It Solves

Modern workspaces often look efficient, but feel draining.

Niuma Detector helps users observe:

- Whether the office environment feels too oppressive
- Whether the chair, desk, lighting, plants, and layout affect comfort
- Whether the user appears tired or low-energy
- Whether they should keep working, take a break, eat better, move, or recover

This is not a medical tool. It is a humorous self-care and workplace wellness demo.

## Core Features

- Camera-based workspace scanning
- Office fatigue vibe detection
- 牛马指数 scoring
- 班味浓度 / 人味余额 / 精神电量
- Workspace wellness rating
- Face-based mood and energy observation
- Playful health, diet, movement, and music suggestions
- Retro game-style UI
- One-click social sharing card generation
- Local-only browser camera processing

## Demo Flow

1. Open the web app
2. Enable camera access
3. Click detect
4. Generate your 牛马指数
5. Read your recovery suggestions
6. Download and share your result card

## Tech Stack

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- WebRTC `getUserMedia`
- Canvas screenshot generation
- Browser-only mock AI analysis

## Privacy

All camera previews are processed locally in the browser.

No image, video, face data, or workspace data is uploaded to any server.

## Disclaimer

This project is for entertainment, self-observation, and creative product prototyping only.

It does not provide medical, psychological, health, or workplace safety diagnosis.
